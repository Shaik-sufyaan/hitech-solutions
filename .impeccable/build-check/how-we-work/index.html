How we work

What to expect when you hire through Hitech Solutions, from the first conversation to a new hire’s first day.

Start with a brief

How the hiring process works.

  1. 1

    Define the need

    Tell us about the role, the skills that matter most, your timeline and how you want to engage. We agree a written brief before searching.

  2. 2

    Review a shortlist

    We present candidates who match the brief, with our notes on experience and fit, and arrange interviews at times that suit you.

  3. 3

    Select and start

    Once you choose, we coordinate the offer and the details needed for a smooth first day, and check in after the start.

Start with a brief

How Hitech Solutions helps employers hire IT professionals.

Tell us the role, the timeline and how you want to engage. We focus the search on people who fit both the work and your team.

What working with us looks like

  • A clear brief before the search begins

    We agree the skills, level, location and terms with you first, so every candidate is measured against the same requirements.

  • A focused shortlist

    You review candidates matched to the brief, with notes on why each one fits, rather than every application received.

  • One contact from shortlist to start date

    Your Hitech contact coordinates interviews, feedback and offers, and stays in touch once the new hire begins.

Questions about the process

All questions
How does the process start?

Send a request with the role and the skills you need. A member of our team will contact you to confirm the brief, the timeline and the terms before the search begins.

How long does it take to see candidates?

It depends on the skills, seniority and location involved. We will give you a realistic estimate when we agree the brief rather than promise a fixed time.

Can you support remote, hybrid and on-site roles?

Tell us where the work happens and how often people need to be on site. We will confirm whether we can support that arrangement when we agree the brief.
