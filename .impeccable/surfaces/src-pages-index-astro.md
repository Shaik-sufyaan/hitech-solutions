# Homepage (/)

Scope: Hitech Solutions homepage. Visitor mode: Persuade — employers decide to request IT talent; job seekers find the route to roles.

Audience and action: employers needing IT professionals → "Find IT talent" (request-talent journey). Secondary: job seekers → "Explore opportunities" (approved careers destination, pending).

Proof and content: no verified metrics, clients, case studies or certifications yet. At the user's request (2026-11-18) the page carries a partner-logo strip, an "at a glance" stats band and three sample client stories as placeholders: stats and stories carry visible Pending markers; the strip is a documented launch blocker. Nothing is presented as verified.

Constraints: see PRODUCT.md Brand Commitments, including the 2026-11-18 approvals (dark middle bands, desktop stacking deck, animated hero, logo strip). Static HTML; JS for nav, FAQ, form feedback, the hero animation, the logo loop and the deck's pin offsets.

Unresolved: service specialties, engagement models, contacts, backend, careers URL, logo/palette.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An established staffing firm that is also unmistakably technical. The hiring journey stays in reference order, but the middle of the page drops into a run of professionally dark panels that stack as you scroll, so the page has a pulse instead of reading as one long white-and-grey list. It refuses SaaS decoration (glows, blobs, gradient text), bubbly radii and fake proof.

OWN-WORLD: Ink navy (#13212E) as the anchor, joined mid-page by steel blue and deep teal panels; a warm amber accent for small marks only (stat bars, footer headings, links and step marks on dark); one action blue for buttons and links on light. Libre Franklin light display, semibold labels; 2px/4px corners; rule-divided lists; rectangular photography.

STORY: Within one viewport an employer sees what Hitech does and how to start; then reads how Hitech helps, scrolls through three stacked dark panels (what roles, how to hire, how it works), sees the firm at a glance and in client stories, and closes on one teal request band.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Unchanged hero: transparent header over the animated navy ASCII field, centred two-line light headline, white "Find IT talent" button, edge-bleeding photographs, the partner strip sitting in the white space below.

FORM: Pinned by the user; signature interaction: the desktop stacking deck (Specialties navy, Ways to hire steel, Process teal), each panel pinning while the next slides over it and the covered panel dimming; plain sections on mobile and reduced motion. seed: none (pinned) — "after third section, when you scroll down it should stay still and sections should move up, like for 2-3 sections" / "some colorful or something like contrast, matching the hero section, not too dark but like professionally dark".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
