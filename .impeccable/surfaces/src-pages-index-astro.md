# Homepage (/)

Scope: Hitech Solutions homepage. Visitor mode: Persuade — employers decide to request IT talent; job seekers find the route to roles.

Audience and action: employers needing IT professionals → "Find IT talent" (request-talent journey). Secondary: job seekers → "Explore opportunities" (approved careers destination, pending).

Proof and content: no verified metrics, clients, case studies or certifications yet. Proof is carried by clarity of process and specifics the business confirms; unsupported sections (case studies, trust strip, insights, office search) are held, not faked.

Constraints: see PRODUCT.md Brand Commitments (binding tone and exclusions). Static HTML; JS only for nav, FAQ, form feedback.

Unresolved: service specialties, engagement models, contacts, backend, careers URL, logo/palette.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An established staffing firm earns trust through order. The page follows the reference's hiring journey — centered statement framed by working photographs, overview, specialties, engagement models, process, FAQ, close — set square-shouldered and quiet. It refuses the SaaS hero (gradient, glow, floating shapes), squircle bubbles, and card grids as the default container.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-navy field (#13212E) for hero and footer; white page; cool paper bands (#F3F5F7); one corporate action blue (#1F4FA8); 1px slate rules (#D8DEE4). Libre Franklin: light display, regular body, semibold labels. Corners 2px on controls, 4px on photographs. Photographs are rectangular, naturally lit, never masked into blobs.

STORY: An employer sees within one viewport that Hitech supplies IT professionals, how to start, and where job seekers go; the page then explains method, specialties and terms in plain language and closes on one request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White 76px header — wordmark left, five text links, "Request talent" button right. Navy hero: centered two-line light headline ~60px within 840px, 20px support copy, white primary button "Find IT talent", candidate line beneath. Two rectangular working photographs bleed off the left (lower) and right (higher) edges. Mobile: one photograph above the headline; CTA visible above the fold.

FORM: Pinned reference structure (Robert Half tech-it) rendered with restraint; signature interaction is functional only — crisp underline/colour hover, visible 2px focus rings, disclosure FAQ. seed: none (pinned) — "Preserve its structure and visual quality, but simplify overly rounded decorative treatments where necessary." / "The website must feel established, clean, restrained, and professional."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
