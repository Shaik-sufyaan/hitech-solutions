# Homepage (/)

Scope: Hitech Solutions homepage. Visitor mode: Persuade — employers decide to request IT talent; job seekers find the route to roles.

Audience and action: employers needing IT professionals → "Find IT talent" (request-talent journey). Secondary: job seekers → "Explore opportunities" (approved careers destination, pending).

Proof and content: no verified metrics, clients, case studies or certifications yet. At the user's request (2026-11-18) the page carries a partner-logo strip, an "at a glance" stats band and three sample client stories as placeholders: stats and stories carry visible Pending markers; the strip is a documented launch blocker. Nothing is presented as verified.

Constraints: see PRODUCT.md Brand Commitments, including the 2026-11-18 approvals (dark middle bands, desktop stacking deck, animated hero, logo strip). Static HTML; JS for nav, FAQ, form feedback, the hero animation, the logo loop and the deck's pin offsets.

Unresolved: service specialties, engagement models, contacts, backend, careers URL, logo/palette.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An established staffing firm that is also unmistakably technical. The hiring journey stays in reference order, but the middle of the page becomes a run of light panels (grey, white, grey) that stack as you scroll, with contrast carried later by dark story cards, a steel Job seekers band, a navy close that mirrors the hero and a navy footer. Dark deck panels were tried and rejected as "dull" and "closed". It refuses SaaS decoration (glows, blobs, gradient text), bubbly radii and fake proof.

OWN-WORLD: Ink navy (#13212E) as the anchor; light grey (#F3F5F7) and white deck panels; steel blue for the Job seekers band; navy for the closing band (teal rejected as clashing); a warm amber accent for small marks only (stat bars, footer headings, links and step marks on dark); one action blue for buttons and links on light. Libre Franklin light display, semibold labels; 2px/4px corners; rule-divided lists; rectangular photography.

STORY: Within one viewport an employer sees what Hitech does and how to start; then reads how Hitech helps, scrolls through three stacked light panels (six specialty rows, how to hire, how it works), sees the firm at a glance and in client stories, and closes on one navy request band that mirrors the hero.

FIRST VIEWPORT (2026-11-19): a full-bleed three-slide photo hero (30 years / Specialists / Ways to hire) under a transparent top bar and navigation that turn white on scroll; each slide has a bold white left-aligned headline, a white lead and its own white button; labelled countdown tabs and a pause button bottom-left; the partner strip sits below.

FORM: Pinned by the user; signature interaction: the desktop stacking deck (Specialties grey, Ways to hire white, Process grey), each panel an inset light sheet (10px corners, hairline border) on a slightly darker bed, pinning one step lower than the last so covered sheets keep a visible edge, shrinking and dimming slightly as the next arrives; plain sections on mobile and reduced motion. seed: none (pinned) — "after third section, when you scroll down it should stay still and sections should move up, like for 2-3 sections" / "some colorful or something like contrast, matching the hero section, not too dark but like professionally dark".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
