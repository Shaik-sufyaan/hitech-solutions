# Hitech Solutions

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), TypeScript, plain CSS with shared design tokens — chosen by the user (2026-11-16). Marketing pages ship as static HTML; JavaScript only for necessary behavior (mobile navigation, FAQ disclosure, form feedback). No React or animation library without a concrete requirement. Employer inquiries must ultimately reach a secure backend (not yet chosen); candidate links must reach an approved careers destination (not yet supplied).

## Users

Primary: employers evaluating IT staffing support — hiring managers, IT leaders and HR/procurement contacts who need technology professionals. Secondary: technology job seekers looking for roles.

## Product Purpose

Hitech Solutions is an IT staffing services company. The website must present the company credibly and move employers to request IT talent, with a clear secondary route for job seekers. Success: qualified employer inquiries that reach the right team, and job seekers reaching the approved careers destination.

## Brand Commitments

- Confirmed company name: Hitech Solutions.
- Structural reference made binding by the user: https://www.roberthalf.com/us/en/tech-it — preserve its structure, professional presentation and clear hiring journey. Never copy Robert Half's branding, claims, awards or testimonials.
- Binding tone (user's words): "established, clean, restrained, and professional." Strong typography, precise alignment, generous consistent spacing, authentic professional photography, a controlled corporate palette, simple section layouts with understated borders, clear navigation and obvious calls to action, mostly static presentation.
- Binding exclusions: floating/moving circles, animated blobs or particles, decorative backgrounds, bouncing cards, parallax, scroll hijacking (native scrolling is never taken over), animated counters, autoplay carousels or hero video, glassmorphism, neon glows, decorative gradients, excessively rounded/bubbly containers, repetitive card grids for every section, generic AI marketing language.
- Approved by the user 2026-11-18 (supersedes the earlier "static, navy only at the ends" rules): (1) the home hero's animated GradientASCII background; (2) a transparent home header that turns solid navy on scroll; (3) a scrolling partner-logo strip; (4) more contrast mid-page: a steel-blue Job seekers band, dark navy client-story cards and a warm amber accent for small marks; (5) a desktop-only "stacking deck" on the home page where three panels pin and the next slides over (CSS sticky on native scroll; plain stacked sections on mobile and under reduced motion). On desktop the deck panels are inset light sheets (grey / white / grey, 10px corners, hairline border) on a slightly darker bed, each pinning a step lower than the last so covered sheets keep a visible edge, and shrinking slightly when covered, so visitors can tell the sections apart. They stay light: dark navy/steel/teal deck panels were built and rejected by the user the same day as "dull" and "compressed and closed". The home Specialties block is distilled to six one-line rows (roles live on the specialty pages); (6) a stats band and sample client stories, each marked Pending until real material exists; (7) a navy closing band (matching the hero; a teal band was tried and rejected as clashing with the theme) and a richer navy footer with an amber top rule.
- Motion otherwise limited to functional feedback (hover, focus, menu, disclosure); always respect reduced motion. Content appears immediately.
- No logo or brand palette exists yet (confirmed 2026-11-16): the site uses a provisional text wordmark and working palette, kept swappable in one token file.
- Primary action is employers requesting IT talent; job seekers get a clear secondary route.
