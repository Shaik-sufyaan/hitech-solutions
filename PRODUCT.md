# Hitech Solutions

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), TypeScript, plain CSS with shared design tokens — chosen by the user (2026-11-16). Marketing pages ship as static HTML; JavaScript only for necessary behavior (mobile navigation, FAQ disclosure, form feedback). No React or animation library without a concrete requirement. Employer inquiries must ultimately reach a secure backend (not yet chosen); candidate links must reach an approved careers destination (not yet supplied).

## Users

Primary: employers evaluating IT staffing support — hiring managers, IT leaders and HR/procurement contacts who need technology professionals. Secondary: technology job seekers looking for roles.

## Product Purpose

Hitech Solutions is an IT staffing services company. The website must present the company credibly and move employers to request IT talent, with a clear secondary route for job seekers. Success: qualified employer inquiries that reach the right team, and job seekers reaching the approved careers destination.

## Brand Commitments

- Confirmed company name: Hitech Solutions.
- Structural reference made binding by the user: https://www.roberthalf.com/us/en/tech-it — preserve its structure, professional presentation and clear hiring journey. Never copy Robert Half's branding, claims, awards or testimonials.
- Binding tone (user's words): "established, clean, restrained, and professional." Strong typography, precise alignment, generous consistent spacing, authentic professional photography, a controlled corporate palette, simple section layouts with understated borders, clear navigation and obvious calls to action, mostly static presentation.
- Binding exclusions: floating/moving circles, animated blobs or particles, decorative backgrounds, bouncing cards, continuously moving elements, parallax, scroll hijacking, animated counters, autoplay carousels or hero video, glassmorphism, neon glows, decorative gradients, excessively rounded/bubbly containers, repetitive card grids for every section, generic AI marketing language.
- Motion limited to functional feedback (hover, focus, menu, disclosure); respect reduced motion. Content appears immediately.
- No logo or brand palette exists yet (confirmed 2026-11-16): the site uses a provisional text wordmark and working palette, kept swappable in one token file.
- Primary action is employers requesting IT talent; job seekers get a clear secondary route.
